## Data-Grounded Strategic Recommendations for BigBasket

1. **Deploy Hyper-Local Marketing Pushes and Catalogue Expansion for Critical Tiers:**
   `Fruits & Vegetables` and `Snacks & Beverages` are facing critical revenue shortfalls (-18% and -16% respectively). The category team should immediately direct direct-to-consumer digital marketing investments, app-banner real estate, and customized bundle promotions toward these zones to recapture baseline velocities.

2. **Conduct Rigorous Supplier Performance and Cold-Chain Supply Logistics Review:**
   Given that both `Dairy & Eggs` (-15%) and `Fruits & Vegetables` (-18%) rely heavily on daily fresh fulfilment logistics, the continuous deficit signals inventory gaps or excessive spoilage. The procurement team must audit local supply metrics, optimize delivery turnarounds, and establish flexible multi-vendor sourcing to stabilize availability.
